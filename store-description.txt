Summary:
Your self-hosted music (Subsonic, Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, Kodi, MPD, Lyrion) in one queue in your panel, playing here or on UPnP/DLNA.

Description:
[b]Streamplay[/b] brings the music on your own servers — several of them at the same time — into a Plasma panel:
[list]
[*][b]Emby[/b]
[*][b]Jellyfin[/b]
[*][b]Kodi[/b]
[*][b]Lyrion Music Server[/b]
[*][b]MPD[/b]
[*][b]Plex[/b]
[*][b]Subsonic[/b]-compatible servers:
  [list]
  [*][b]Airsonic[/b]
  [*][b]Ampache[/b]
  [*][b]Funkwhale[/b]
  [*][b]Gonic[/b]
  [*][b]LMS[/b]
  [*][b]Navidrome[/b]
  [/list]
[/list]

Browse everything as one library, build a single queue from all of it, and choose where it plays:
[list]
[*][b]This computer[/b]
[*][b]Kodi[/b]
[*][b]Lyrion[/b] players (Squeezebox and compatible)
[*][b]MPD[/b]
[*][b]UPnP/DLNA[/b] speakers, TVs and receivers
[/list]

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*][b]All your servers at once.[/b] Each one has its own on/off switch. The library merges them, and a badge on every row shows where an item came from.
[*][b]One queue for everything.[/b] A Subsonic album and a Kodi album can sit side by side and play one after the other.
[*][b]Choose where it plays.[/b] Play on the local speakers, on Kodi, MPD, Lyrion players or UPnP/DLNA renderers such as Sonos, and switch in the middle of a track without losing your place.
[*][b]Gapless playback[/b] on this computer, with the next track buffered in advance.
[*][b]Browse[/b] by album, artist, genre or playlist, and search across every connected server.
[*][b]Edit the queue.[/b] Play next, add to the end, replace, drag to reorder, remove, shuffle and three repeat modes.
[*][b]Works with the rest of KDE.[/b] Media keys, the Media Player widget, the lock screen and the volume OSD all control it through MPRIS.
[*][b]Passwords stay in your keyring[/b] (KWallet, GNOME Keyring or KeePassXC via the Secret Service) and never go into a config file.
[/list]

[b]Keyboard[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Ctrl+1 / 2 / 3[/b] — Playing / Queue / Library tab
[*][b]Ctrl+H / Ctrl+L[/b] — previous / next tab, also while typing in the search field
[*][b]Ctrl+F[/b] — search the library, from any tab
[*][b]↓ / ↑[/b], [b]Ctrl+N / P[/b], [b]Ctrl+J / K[/b] — move through the library; from the search field into the results and back
[*][b]Enter[/b] — open the artist, album, genre or playlist; play a track
[*][b]Ctrl+Enter[/b] — play now, replacing the queue
[*][b]Shift+Enter[/b] — add to the queue
[*][b]Backspace[/b] — back
[/list]

[b]How it works: the background service[/b]
A Plasma widget cannot play audio or register as a media player by itself, so Streamplay comes in two parts. The widget you see is only the interface. A small Python service does the playing, and it is [b]bundled inside this download[/b], so there is nothing else to fetch.

[list=1]
[*]Add the widget to a panel. The first time, it shows a [b]Start Service[/b] button.
[*]Clicking it starts the bundled service as a systemd user unit ([i]streamplay-applet[/i]). It runs as you, only on 127.0.0.1, and writes nothing outside your home folder. No root access is needed.
[*]After that, the widget starts the service whenever it finds it not running, for example after you log in.
[*]The service runs separately from plasmashell, so [b]your music keeps playing when Plasma restarts[/b].
[*]After a store update, the widget sees that the service is an older version and restarts it.
[/list]

[b]Dependencies[/b]
The service relies on a few common packages from your distribution. Before starting it, the widget checks for them. If anything is missing, it shows [b]exactly which packages to install[/b] and has a [i]Check Again[/i] button, so you never have to guess from a vague error.

[list]
[*][b]Arch / Manjaro:[/b] python-requests python-websockets python-secretstorage python-dbus python-gobject mpv
[*][b]Debian / Ubuntu / KDE neon:[/b] python3-requests python3-websockets python3-secretstorage python3-dbus python3-gi mpv
[*][b]Fedora:[/b] python3-requests python3-websockets python3-secretstorage python3-dbus python3-gobject mpv (mpv comes from RPM Fusion)
[*][b]openSUSE:[/b] python3-requests python3-websockets python3-SecretStorage python3-dbus-python python3-gobject mpv
[/list]
mpv plays the audio on this computer, and python-dbus plus python-gobject provide the media-key and Now Playing integration. Requires Plasma 6.

[b]Getting started[/b]
Open the widget's settings, go to [i]Music Servers[/i] and choose [i]Add Music Server…[/i]. [i]Test Connection[/i] checks your settings before you save them. For Kodi, turn on [i]Settings → Services → Control → Allow remote control via HTTP[/i] (and allow control from other systems).

[b]Removing it[/b]
To stop the service: [i]systemctl --user stop streamplay-applet[/i]. It no longer starts once the widget is removed. Your servers and settings stay in ~/.config/streamplay/.

[b]Prefer a permanent install?[/b] The GitHub repository has an install script that sets the service up as a regular systemd user service: [url=https://github.com/leissa/streamplay]github.com/leissa/streamplay[/url]. Bug reports and ideas are welcome there too.
